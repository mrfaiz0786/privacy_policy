01

Purpose and Applicability

This policy governs the registration, verification, onboarding, conduct, delivery responsibilities, cash handling, safety, payments and termination of all delivery partners associated with VR YBM Private Limited and YourBuildMart or YBM.

The policy applies to independent delivery partners operating:

  1. Two-wheelers (motorcycles and scooters)
  2. Three-wheelers (cargo autos and other permitted goods carriers)
  3. Four-wheelers (commercial delivery vans, pickups and other permitted goods vehicles)

All delivery partners must accept this policy digitally before receiving any delivery assignment.

Registration does not guarantee approval, activation or a minimum number of delivery assignments.

02

Eligibility and Mandatory Registration

Every applicant must satisfy the following conditions:

  1. Be at least 18 years of age and legally eligible to operate the proposed vehicle.
  2. Possess a valid driving licence appropriate to the vehicle category.
  3. Possess valid vehicle registration, insurance and all permits, fitness certificates and other documents required by applicable law.
  4. Have a functioning smartphone with internet access, GPS and camera.
  5. Maintain an active bank account in their own name.
  6. Complete identity verification, facial verification and vehicle verification.
  7. Provide accurate personal, contact and vehicle information.
  8. Pass the Company’s onboarding and verification checks.

Mandatory documents:

  1. Government-issued identity proof
  2. PAN or other legally accepted tax documentation, where applicable
  3. Valid driving licence
  4. Vehicle registration certificate
  5. Valid motor insurance
  6. Commercial permit, fitness certificate and other statutory documents, wherever required
  7. Bank account details and verification
  8. Live selfie for identity matching
  9. Emergency contact details

Aadhaar-based verification, where used, shall follow applicable consent, authentication and data-protection requirements. The Company shall not unnecessarily retain full Aadhaar numbers.

YBM reserves the right to reject or suspend applications containing false, incomplete, expired or unverifiable information.

03

Verification and Activation

No delivery partner may collect an order until their account has been approved and activated.

The onboarding process shall include:

  1. Mobile OTP verification.
  2. Identity and facial verification.
  3. Driving licence and vehicle document verification.
  4. Bank account verification.
  5. Mandatory safety and delivery training.
  6. Acceptance of this Rider Policy.
  7. Final account activation by YBM.

YBM may conduct periodic re-verification and require updated documents.

Accounts may be restricted immediately if mandatory documents expire or verification fails.

04

Independent Delivery Partner Status

Riders shall be engaged as independent delivery service partners under a separate service arrangement, subject to applicable laws.

The platform delivery service window is 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Indian Standard Time), subject to operational changes notified by YBM. Independent riders may choose their availability and accept or decline offers within that window, subject to platform rules. This window does not create a mandatory 12-hour shift, employment relationship or guaranteed work. No collection or delivery outside the window is authorised without express approval.

The arrangement does not automatically create an employment relationship. However, statutory obligations, social security entitlements and other legal rights shall apply wherever required by law.

Riders shall be responsible for their own vehicle maintenance, fuel, traffic compliance and other operating expenses unless specifically agreed otherwise.

05

Vehicle Categories and Delivery Eligibility

Delivery assignments shall be allocated according to vehicle suitability, payload capacity, dimensions, route and customer requirements.

a) Two-wheelers

Permitted for small, lightweight and securely packed items such as electrical accessories, plumbing fittings, small hardware and compatible packaged materials.

b) Three-wheelers

Permitted for medium-sized consignments such as paint buckets, hardware cartons, packaged chemicals and other goods within the vehicle’s permitted capacity.

c) Four-wheelers

Permitted for larger consignments including plywood, boards, construction materials, larger paint quantities and other bulky products.

Riders must never transport goods exceeding their vehicle’s legally permitted load, dimensions or safety capacity.

The Company may reassign orders if the registered vehicle is unsuitable.

06

Order Acceptance and Pickup

Once a delivery partner accepts an order:

  1. The rider must proceed to the assigned dark store within the applicable pickup window.
  2. The rider must present the order ID or pickup authentication code.
  3. The rider must verify the number of packages and visible packaging condition.
  4. The rider must ensure that the material can be safely loaded and transported.
  5. The rider must report visible shortages, damaged packaging or incorrect packages before leaving.
  6. Pickup confirmation must be completed through the Rider App.
  7. The rider must not leave the dark store before the pickup has been digitally confirmed.

The store team shall be responsible for preparing and verifying the order contents. The rider shall be responsible for the packages acknowledged at pickup, subject to documented condition, sealed-package limitations and applicable law.

Any discrepancy must be reported before departure.

07

Delivery Timelines and Tracking

  1. GPS tracking must remain enabled during an active delivery.
  2. Riders must follow lawful, safe and reasonably efficient routes.
  3. Unauthorised diversion, deliberate delay or order abandonment is prohibited.
  4. Riders must notify YBM immediately of breakdowns, traffic disruptions, accidents or other delivery difficulties.
  5. Delivery estimates are targets and shall never require unsafe driving or violation of traffic laws.

YBM may monitor active delivery progress for operational and safety purposes in accordance with its privacy policy.

08

Cash on Delivery (COD) Policy

The maximum Cash on Delivery order value shall be ₹2,000 per order.

The following conditions are mandatory:

  1. COD is permitted only for orders with a total customer-payable value of ₹2,000 or less.
  2. Orders exceeding ₹2,000 must be prepaid through an approved payment method before dispatch.
  3. Riders may collect only the exact amount displayed in the Rider App.
  4. Riders must not demand additional delivery charges, tips or other payments.
  5. Cash must not be transferred to any unauthorised person.
  6. The rider must confirm the cash collected in the Rider App immediately.
  7. COD orders must be marked completed only after successful delivery and confirmation of payment.
  8. All collected cash remains payable to YBM and must be settled through an authorised process.

COD settlement

  1. Cash collected must be deposited or handed over through the Company’s approved settlement process.
  2. The normal settlement deadline shall be the end of the rider’s delivery session or another deadline expressly displayed in the app.
  3. A rider with overdue or unaccounted COD funds may be temporarily restricted from accepting further COD orders.
  4. Missing cash, false payment confirmations or deliberate diversion of customer funds may result in account suspension, recovery proceedings and lawful action.
  5. The Company may impose a lower individual COD exposure limit based on the rider’s verification status, transaction history and risk assessment.
09

Delivery Confirmation and Proof of Delivery

Every successful delivery must be authenticated through the Rider App.

Mandatory requirements:

  1. GPS-confirmed arrival at the delivery location.
  2. Customer delivery OTP or another Company-approved authentication method.
  3. Photograph of the delivered packages, where required.
  4. Recording of delivery completion time.
  5. COD collection confirmation, where applicable.

The rider must not mark an order delivered before actual handover.

Customer OTPs must not be requested before delivery.

Photographs must not unnecessarily expose customers, children, private interiors or sensitive information.

10

Damage, Shortage and Material Handling

Riders must handle all goods carefully.

The following actions are strictly prohibited:

  1. Opening sealed packages without authorisation.
  2. Tampering with products or packaging.
  3. Substituting or removing materials.
  4. Leaving packages unattended.
  5. Transporting goods in unsafe or unsecured conditions.
  6. Mixing incompatible chemicals or carrying prohibited hazardous goods.
  7. Using goods for personal purposes.

Any damage, leakage, loss or shortage must be reported immediately through the app, with supporting photographs.

A rider is financially responsible for verified loss, theft, shortage, damage, misdelivery or unauthorised disposal of packages entrusted to them to the extent caused by their negligence, wilful misconduct, breach of this Policy or unlawful acts. YBM may seek recovery of the reasonably documented actual loss, including substantiated replacement, refund or return costs, after reviewing pickup records, packaging condition, evidence and the rider’s explanation. Riders are not responsible for pre-existing shortages, concealed packing defects or events beyond their legal responsibility. No arbitrary penalty or unlawful wage deduction is authorised.

11

Failed Delivery, Customer Refusal and Return to Store

If a delivery cannot be completed, the rider must select the appropriate issue in the Rider App:

  1. Customer unavailable
  2. Customer refuses delivery
  3. Incorrect or inaccessible address
  4. Customer refuses COD payment
  5. Damaged package
  6. Incomplete or incorrect order
  7. Vehicle breakdown or accident
  8. Other operational issue

Photographic or other appropriate evidence must be uploaded where required.

The rider must contact YBM support and follow the approved return instructions.

No undelivered order may be retained overnight, transferred to another person or taken to an unauthorised location without written or in-app approval.

Returned materials must be verified and acknowledged by the dark store.

12

Rider Conduct and Customer Interaction

All riders must maintain professional behaviour.

Strictly prohibited conduct includes:

  1. Verbal abuse, threats, harassment or discrimination.
  2. Consuming alcohol or intoxicants during an active delivery, or driving while impaired by alcohol, narcotics, drugs or other intoxicating substances. Zero tolerance applies; the rider is personally responsible for their unlawful conduct and attributable consequences.
  3. Demanding unauthorised payments.
  4. Sharing customer contact details or addresses.
  5. Contacting customers for personal purposes.
  6. Sending another person to complete deliveries using the registered account.
  7. Fraudulent GPS manipulation or fake delivery confirmation.
  8. Theft, misappropriation or intentional damage.
  9. Operating without valid documents or legally required safety equipment.
  10. Using a different vehicle category without updating and obtaining approval in the app.
  11. Riders must comply with all applicable traffic, transport and safety regulations.
13

Account Security and Anti-Fraud Rules

Each Rider App account is personal and non-transferable.

The rider must not:

  1. Share login credentials or OTPs.
  2. Rent, sell or transfer their account.
  3. Use another person’s identity.
  4. Operate multiple fraudulent accounts.
  5. Manipulate location, delivery status or payment records.
  6. Submit forged documents.
  7. Collude with customers, store personnel or other riders to defraud YBM.

YBM may temporarily suspend an account while investigating suspected fraud or safety violations.

14

Earnings, Payments and Settlements

Delivery charges and applicable incentives shall be displayed in the Rider App or communicated through an approved rate schedule.

The rider shall be able to review:

  1. Completed deliveries
  2. Delivery earnings
  3. Applicable incentives
  4. Approved adjustments
  5. COD collections and settlements
  6. Pending payouts
  7. Payment history

Payments shall be made to the verified bank account according to the Company’s notified settlement cycle.

Any deductions or recoveries must have a documented basis, comply with applicable law and be subject to a dispute mechanism.

YBM does not guarantee minimum earnings or delivery volumes unless separately agreed.

15

Performance Standards and Quality Monitoring

YBM may evaluate delivery partners using objective operational parameters, including:

  1. Pickup punctuality
  2. Successful delivery completion
  3. Customer complaints
  4. Damage and loss incidents
  5. COD settlement compliance
  6. Verified fraud or misconduct
  7. Safety compliance
  8. Proper use of the Rider App

Ratings and automated risk flags shall not alone establish misconduct.

Riders must have an opportunity to report errors and challenge material adverse decisions.

Repeated verified violations may lead to training requirements, temporary restrictions or account termination.

16

Suspension and Termination

YBM may immediately restrict access where reasonably necessary to address:

  1. Suspected theft or fraud
  2. Unaccounted COD collections
  3. Serious safety violations
  4. False identity or forged documents
  5. Account sharing
  6. Threats or harassment
  7. Significant risk to customer property or personal safety

For other breaches, YBM may issue warnings, require corrective training or impose proportionate restrictions.

Permanent termination shall generally follow documented review, notice of the grounds and an opportunity to respond, except where immediate action is legally required or necessary to prevent serious harm.

Termination does not extinguish lawful outstanding payment, return or settlement obligations.

17

Insurance, Safety and Legal Compliance

Riders must independently obtain and maintain, at their own expense, valid vehicle insurance appropriate to the vehicle and commercial use (where required), driving authorisations, registration, permits, fitness and pollution certificates. Riders must also arrange their own suitable personal accident, disability and death insurance coverage. Expired or inadequate cover is grounds for immediate deactivation until corrected.

YBM does not provide or promise vehicle, personal accident, life, death, disability or medical insurance under this Policy. Each rider must independently procure, pay for and maintain valid legally required motor insurance and appropriate personal accident/death and disability cover at their own cost. Any insurance or statutory benefit expressly required by applicable law remains unaffected. YBM is not the insurer and does not guarantee payment of claims.

Riders must use required protective equipment, follow load-restraint standards and comply with applicable restrictions on commercial vehicles and transportation of regulated materials.

The Company shall comply with applicable gig-worker, platform-worker and social-security requirements as they become enforceable.

Nothing in this Policy excludes mandatory obligations under Indian motor vehicle, labour, social security, consumer, privacy or other applicable laws, nor limits liability that cannot legally be excluded. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply. This Policy should be read together with the separate Rider Partner Agreement and applicable Privacy Notice.

17F. Statutory Rights and Severability

Any accident, police stop, challan affecting delivery, breakdown, suspected theft, missing parcel, damage, customer confrontation or COD discrepancy must be reported immediately in the Rider App or to YBM support, with photographs and relevant records where safe and lawful. YBM may preserve GPS, pickup scans, OTP logs, photographs and communications for a proportionate investigation. Before recovery, YBM shall provide the rider with a summary of the allegation, evidence and proposed calculation and a reasonable opportunity to respond. Disputed amounts shall be reviewed through the grievance process.

17E. Incident Reporting, Evidence and Fair Assessment

The rider must not share their account, substitute a driver, carry unauthorised passengers, subcontract deliveries, combine YBM goods with unrelated cargo where unsafe, leave goods unattended, use a phone while driving unlawfully, carry goods beyond the vehicle’s legal capacity, transport prohibited materials, or disclose customer information. Riders must use appropriate protective equipment and securing straps; report damaged seals, leaks and unsafe loading before departure; and must not sign for packages not actually received. Riders must not accept cash outside the authorised COD process or use personal payment QR codes.

17D. Operational Safeguards and Prohibited Practices

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the rider shall indemnify and hold harmless VR YBM Private Limited, its officers and personnel against reasonably evidenced third-party claims, damages, penalties, losses and reasonable legal expenses to the extent directly arising from the rider’s negligence, fraud, theft, reckless or intoxicated driving, unlawful acts, breach of traffic or transport law, unauthorised delegation, or material breach of this Policy. No indemnity applies to the extent a loss results from YBM’s own negligence, unlawful act, directions or other non-excludable liability. YBM may demand repayment of verified rider-attributable losses and pursue lawful recovery; set-off against earnings is permitted only where legally allowed, transparently itemised and subject to a dispute process.

17C. Indemnity and Recovery

The rider is responsible for lawful and safe operation of their vehicle, including helmet/seat-belt use, vehicle roadworthiness, secure loading and compliance with speed limits. In the event of an accident, injury, death, property damage or third-party claim, the rider must first seek emergency assistance, notify police or authorities where required, inform YBM promptly and preserve evidence. The rider must report incidents to their own insurer and pursue claims under their own policies. YBM does not assume responsibility for the rider’s personal injury, death or vehicle damage merely by providing a delivery assignment, except to the extent liability or benefits are imposed by law or result from YBM’s own actionable conduct.

17B. Accidents, Injury, Death and Third-Party Claims

All traffic fines, police challans, penalties, towing, impoundment, licence violations, parking offences, permit breaches, overloading offences and related charges arising from the rider’s acts or omissions are the rider’s responsibility. The rider must promptly inform YBM of any incident affecting an assigned order, cooperate with lawful investigations and never represent that YBM will pay a fine. This allocation does not override any liability that applicable law places on YBM for its own conduct.

Data Privacy and Consent

YBM may collect and process information necessary for onboarding, identity verification, delivery management, payment settlement, fraud prevention and safety.

This may include identification details, vehicle information, bank details, location data during active delivery and delivery records.

Data processing shall follow applicable Indian data-protection laws and the Company’s privacy notice.

Riders shall be informed of the purposes of collection, retention periods, grievance channels and applicable rights.

19

Complaints, Appeals and Dispute Resolution

Riders may submit complaints regarding:

  1. Payment discrepancies
  2. Incorrect COD balances
  3. Account restrictions
  4. Customer complaints
  5. Damage or loss assessments
  6. Incorrect performance records

YBM shall provide an in-app support and escalation mechanism, with documented decisions and reasonable response timelines.

Any dispute-resolution and jurisdiction provisions shall be specified in the separate Rider Partner Agreement and remain subject to mandatory applicable law.

20

Policy Amendments

YBM may update this policy for operational, regulatory, safety or security reasons.

Material changes affecting rider obligations, payments or eligibility shall be communicated with reasonable advance notice unless urgent implementation is required.

Continued use after a validly notified effective date may constitute acceptance where legally permissible.

22

Location Tracking and Permissions

The YBM Rider App may request access to device location services for delivery-related operations.

  • GPS tracking is required during active deliveries.
  • Location data may be used to monitor delivery progress and confirm arrival.
  • Location access may support navigation, route planning and delivery coordination.
  • Riders will be informed when location permissions are requested.

Any background location tracking, if implemented, must be separately disclosed and limited to the purposes and permissions communicated to riders.

23

Camera and Photo Permissions

The application may request camera access for rider identity verification, verification selfies, proof-of-delivery photographs, damage reports and delivery-related incident evidence.

Photographs should not unnecessarily expose customers, children, private interiors or sensitive personal information.

24

Data Sharing and Disclosure

YBM may share necessary personal information with authorised personnel and service providers for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Authorised YBM operations and support teams
  • Identity and document verification providers
  • Payment and banking service providers
  • Technology and hosting service providers
  • Government authorities or law enforcement where legally required

YBM will apply appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards to protect shared information.

25

Data Retention and Deletion

YBM will retain rider information only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, applicable legal obligations, operational records, payment reconciliation and legitimate dispute resolution.

When personal information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymised or otherwise securely disposed of, subject to applicable law. Specific retention periods and account deletion procedures will be communicated through the applicable privacy notice or support process.

26

Rider Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Indian data-protection laws, eligible riders may request:

  • Information about personal data being processed
  • Correction or updating of inaccurate information
  • Erasure of information where legally applicable
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent
  • Resolution of privacy-related grievances

Requests may be submitted through YBM's designated privacy or support channel.
